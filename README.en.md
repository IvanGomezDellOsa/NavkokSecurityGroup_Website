# NavkokSecurityGroup — Corporate Website

Complete redesign of the corporate website for **Navkok Security Group SRL**, a private security company with over 30 years of experience and ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 certifications. Freelance project: from the initial proposal to the production deploy.

🌐 **Production deploy:** [navkok-website-private.vercel.app](https://navkok-website-private.vercel.app/)

---

## 🛠 Stack

`Next.js 16` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS v4` `shadcn/ui` `Vercel`

---

## 🎨 Design and Assets

The site uses a dark visual system with a golden accent inspired by elite security aesthetics. The palette, typography and components were defined from scratch to reflect the company's professionalism.

The hero background video was generated with AI tools and processed with FFmpeg for compression and cropping, prioritizing the smallest possible weight without sacrificing visual quality. The site's images were optimized in batch with Sharp, achieving reductions of up to 90% in weight compared to the original files.

---

## 📝 Development Notes

Freelance project with direct client contact. I gathered requirements, proposed the content architecture and the visual approach, wrote the institutional copy and incorporated my own material — including images and video generated with AI. Development was assisted by LLMs to speed up layout and component writing. The product, design and content strategy decisions were made and orchestrated by me throughout the whole cycle.
